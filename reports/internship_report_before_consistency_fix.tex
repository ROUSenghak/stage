\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This report is the \textbf{quick-reading guide} to the internship: it is meant
to let a reader scan the project rapidly and understand \emph{what was done},
\emph{why it matters}, and \emph{where each workstream sits} in the overall
pipeline. The detailed implementation, audit logic, intermediate diagnostics,
and methodological justifications are intentionally left to the separate
\textbf{technical report} (\texttt{phase1\_technical\_report.pdf}).

The internship work concerns predictive analysis of public digital procurement
for \textbf{Gigalis}, a central purchasing body in Pays de la Loire. The goal
is to move from a descriptive view (``what happened?'') towards a predictive one
(``when will a contract be re-tendered?''). Because BOAMP contains
\emph{no explicit renewal field}, the renewal relationship must be reconstructed
from observable signals. We build a proxy renewal event using
Sentence-Transformer text embeddings combined with CPV, temporal, and buyer
signals, and use it as the event variable for survival analysis. On
1{,}100 eligible source contracts the linking method recovers a plausible renewal
for 697 of them (\textbf{63.4\%}), against an 11.3\% lexical baseline. Survival
modeling gives a global Kaplan--Meier median survival of \textbf{48.1 months},
a Cox concordance of \textbf{0.6524}, and a best-fitting log-normal parametric
model. The fitted model is then translated into 12- and 24-month renewal-risk
indicators at contract, buyer, and segment level.
\end{abstract}

\section{Context and Problem}

\subsection{Why this project}
Gigalis operates a central purchasing body for digital services in Pays de la
Loire. To anticipate re-tendering workloads, it wants to predict \emph{when}
digital contracts come up for renewal. The core obstacle: BOAMP publishes
calls for tenders and awards, but \emph{no notice says ``this renews contract X''}.
The renewal link must be inferred from text, classification, timing, and buyer
identity.

\subsection{Scope}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Sectors:} CPV divisions 48 (software), 72 (IT services), 32 (telecom), 35 (security).
  \item \textbf{Territory:} departments 44, 49, 53, 72, 85 (Pays de la Loire).
  \item \textbf{Period:} 2015--2024. Study end date: 2024-12-31.
  \item \textbf{Modeling path:} BOAMP only. DECP is documented but not linked
        (no shared key between the two sources).
\end{itemize}

\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Corpus numbers}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item 3,181 BOAMP notices downloaded (2015--2024).
  \item 1,933 are \texttt{APPEL\_OFFRE} (source contracts); 1,081 are \texttt{ATTRIBUTION}.
  \item 1,100 are \emph{eligible}: their expected renewal window falls before 2024-12-31.
  \item 833 are structurally censored upfront (too recent to observe a renewal).
\end{itemize}

DECP (3,039 digital contracts, same scope) is far richer (100\% SIRET,
99.4\% durations) but cannot be merged to BOAMP at contract level.
It remains a future enrichment path.

\subsection{Taxonomy}
Each contract is tagged with one of eleven technology categories
(Software \& Applications, IT Services \& Consulting, Telecom \& Networks,
Cybersecurity, Cloud \& Infrastructure, Data \& AI, etc.) via a
\textbf{deterministic rule-based mapping} from CPV codes and description keywords.
This is \emph{not} a trained classifier --- the planned NLP classifier was not
implemented. Output: \nolinkurl{data/processed/taxonomy.csv}.

\section{What Was Built}

\subsection{Phase 1 --- Renewal-event reconstruction}
\label{sec:linking}

Each eligible source contract is matched against later BOAMP notices from the
same buyer using a four-signal composite score: semantic text embeddings
(Sentence-Transformer, model \nolinkurl{paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2}),
CPV hierarchy match, temporal proximity, and buyer-identity agreement
(weights 0.40 / 0.25 / 0.20 / 0.15). The best candidate above the hard
filter threshold is selected as the renewal successor.
See technical report \S3.2 for the full formula and weight derivation.

\textbf{Link quality.} Of the 697 accepted links: 135 are single-candidate
(no competing match); of the remaining 562 multi-candidate links, 273 (49\%)
have a score margin below 0.05, flagging an ambiguous winner. Only 53 links
(7.6\%) satisfy the strict high-confidence criterion (score $\geq 0.80$ and
margin $\geq 0.05$). This low high-confidence rate motivates the manual
validation step (not yet done; see Section~\ref{sec:open}).

\textbf{Main unlinked failure mode.} 339 of 403 unlinked contracts (84\%) have
no temporal partner in the search window --- not a scoring failure, but a true
absence of a candidate notice in the observable period.

Outputs: \nolinkurl{boamp_renewal_linking_quality/outputs/} (\nolinkurl{boamp_renewal_links.csv},
\nolinkurl{boamp_renewal_candidates.csv}, \nolinkurl{boamp_linking_stats.csv}).

\subsection{Phase 2 --- Survival analysis}
\label{sec:survival}

The survival dataset (\nolinkurl{data/processed/boamp_phase2_survival.csv}) has
one row per eligible contract with observed/censored duration and covariates
(\nolinkurl{declared_duration_months}, \nolinkurl{dur_was_imputed}, \texttt{start\_year},
category, procedure type). Three model families were fitted in
\nolinkurl{notebooks/02_survival_modeling_boamp.ipynb}.

\paragraph{Kaplan--Meier.} Global median survival: \textbf{48.1 months},
consistent with the prevalence of 48-month framework contracts.
Log-rank tests (Table~\ref{tab:logrank}): renewal timing is driven by
declared duration and imputation status ($p<0.001$), not by technology category
($p=0.15$).

\begin{table}[H]
\centering

\midrule

Declared-duration group        & 62.43 & $<0.001$ & 2 \\

\paragraph{Cox proportional-hazards model.} In-sample concordance:
\textbf{0.6524}. Two predictors are significant (Table~\ref{tab:cox}): longer
declared duration lowers hazard (HR 0.991 per month), and later start year
raises it (HR 1.094 per year). No category or procedure effect.

The positive \texttt{start\_year} HR is partly structural: 2021--2024 contracts
are right-censored because a typical 48-month contract would renew after the
2024-12-31 study close.

\paragraph{Parametric AFT models.} Log-normal fits best (AIC 7,119.8,
concordance 0.672), outperforming log-logistic (7,194.3) and Weibull (7,320.3).
Full parametric results: \nolinkurl{reports/tables/survival/parametric_aic_comparison.csv}.

\paragraph{Sensitivity analysis.} Three event definitions were tested
(Table~\ref{tab:sens}). The Cox concordance is stable across all three,
confirming that the \emph{ranking} of renewal risk is robust even under the
strictest definition.

\paragraph{Proportional hazards assumption.} Schoenfeld test flags a violation
for \nolinkurl{declared_duration_months} ($p < 10^{-5}$); all other covariates
pass. Stratification or a time interaction for duration is flagged as future
work. The log-normal AFT avoids the PH assumption and gives the same conclusions.

\subsection{Robustness and validation checks}
\label{sec:robustness}

These are in \nolinkurl{validation_robustness/} (untracked --- results exist but
not merged to main git history yet).

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Threshold sensitivity (7 scenarios, score 0.35 to 0.90):}
        Cox concordance stays above 0.59 throughout; directional findings stable.
        Full table: \nolinkurl{validation_robustness/outputs/threshold_sensitivity_summary.csv}.
  \item \textbf{Placebo check:} winning link mean score 0.579 vs.\ runner-up
        0.435 vs.\ synthetic no-buyer 0.425 --- scoring discriminates.
  \item \textbf{Event/censoring bias:} SMD = 0.13 (duration), $-$0.03 (start year)
        between event and censored groups --- covariates balanced; dominant
        bias is structural right-censoring of 2022--2024 contracts.
  \item \textbf{Manual validation:} 150-row sample drawn
        (\nolinkurl{event_validation/outputs/manual_validation_sample.csv}),
        protocol documented (\nolinkurl{event_validation/event_validation_protocol.md}).
        \textbf{Record-level review not yet executed.}
        Precision/recall of the linking step therefore unknown.
\end{itemize}

\section{What Was Not Done}
\label{sec:open}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Manual validation of linked pairs.} The 150-row sample is ready;
        the review has not been executed. Until done, there are no ground-truth
        precision / recall estimates for the linking step.
  \item \textbf{Trained NLP classifier.} The technology taxonomy is rule-based
        (CPV + keywords). The planned learned classifier was not implemented.
  \item \textbf{Change-point / trend detection (Phase 4).} Scoped but not
        started. No code or outputs exist.
  \item \textbf{DECP semantic linking.} The Sentence-Transformer method was
        applied to BOAMP only. DECP linking remains at the 8.3\% lexical
        prototype.
  \item \textbf{Live prediction scoring.} The current model evaluates contract
        age as of the study end date. A production run would need to re-evaluate
        each contract at a live prediction date.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Limitations}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Proxy event.} \texttt{event = 1} means a plausible renewal link
        was found, not a legally certified renewal. Most links are low-to-medium
        confidence (48.6\% low, 24.4\% medium, 7.6\% high).
  \item \textbf{Weak buyer identification.} Only $\sim$13\% of buyers carry a
        SIRET; the rest are matched by normalised name. Spelling variants
        (``Ville de Nantes'' vs ``Nantes M\'etropole'') create false negatives.
        The 2024 eForms notices do include SIRET but are all censored upfront.
  \item \textbf{Short-text false positives.} Generic descriptions
        (``prestations informatiques'') can match spuriously.
  \item \textbf{Structural right-censoring.} Contracts from 2021--2024 are
        largely censored; the positive start\_year hazard partly reflects study
        truncation, not a genuine cohort trend.
  \item \textbf{Modest discrimination.} Concordance $\approx$0.65 --- useful
        for prioritisation, not sufficient for high-stakes individual decisions.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion and Next Steps}

The internship delivered a reproducible end-to-end pipeline: BOAMP notice
acquisition $\to$ cleaning $\to$ renewal-event reconstruction $\to$ survival
modeling $\to$ operational risk indicators. The main methodological result is
lifting the renewal linking rate from 11.3\% (lexical baseline) to 63.4\%
using Sentence-Transformer embeddings. Survival modeling shows renewal timing is
driven by declared contract duration (median 48.1 months) rather than
technology family, and the log-normal AFT model translates this into
actionable 12/24-month renewal-risk rankings.

\textbf{Immediate next steps (prioritised):}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Execute manual validation} of the 150-row sample to get
        ground-truth precision/recall for the linking step
        (\texttt{event\_validation/}).
  \item \textbf{Re-score contracts at a live prediction date} (current scores
        use end-of-study ages).
  \item \textbf{SIRET/SIREN enrichment} to strengthen buyer resolution.
  \item \textbf{Replace rule-based taxonomy} with a trained text classifier.
  \item \textbf{Implement change-point detection} (Phase 4).
\end{enumerate}
